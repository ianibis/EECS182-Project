# Archived MLP results

These CSVs were copied from the established Box runs. Training summaries
are separated by OFF, recorded 1× ON, and 10× ON checkpoint. Regular
evaluation, calibration ablation, and OFF-only evaluation have separate
validation and test folders.

The OFF-trained checkpoint gives mean OFF R² of 0.186438 on validation and
0.206771 on test. See [Task 4](../../task4/README.md) for commands, external
inputs, and the 20-trial same-day OFF adaptation protocol.
